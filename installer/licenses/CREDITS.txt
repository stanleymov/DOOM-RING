DOOM RING - credits
===================

DOOM RING is an unofficial fan mod, made with AI help (Claude by Anthropic). It is not affiliated
with or endorsed by id Software, Bethesda, FromSoftware or Bandai Namco.
DOOM Eternal (c) id Software / Bethesda. ELDEN RING (c) FromSoftware / Bandai Namco.
No game files are included: the setup builds DOOM RING's DOOM content from your own DOOM Eternal.
